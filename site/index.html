01

Your salon starts keeping a record.

A dental practice opens your chart before you sit down. NailScan gives your salon the same thing: every scan writes a dated, structured record of what her nails actually looked like — surface, colour, structure, cuticle — and the care plan she was given that day.

Six visits later, that is a history. You can see her nails getting stronger, which service actually did it, and who is due back this week. The scanner is the instrument. The record is the asset.

One nail, five frames. This is the whole assessment: the frame she submits, the frame that survives admission, and the three passes that put something on the record. Nothing here is a filter — every mark is a field the front desk can read back.

EVERY SCANA dated record with five structured fields, the readings derived from them, and the plan she was given — not a PDF, a row you can query.
EVERY CLIENTOne file, matched on phone number, so a returning client builds a history instead of forking into three half-empty contacts.
EVERY EXPORTYours, in full, on request, at any time — including after you stop working with us. Nothing is held back as leverage.

This is the part that compounds. A scanner you can buy anywhere. A named, condition-tagged history of every client who has ever walked through your door is infrastructure, it belongs to you, and it is worth more in year three than it was on the day it was installed.
